Outlook Troubleshooting

Problem:
Outlook is not syncing emails properly.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check the internet connection.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. Check whether Outlook is in Offline mode.
4. Sign out and sign in again.
5. Restart the computer.
6. If the problem continues, contact Service Desk.

Resolution:
Restart Outlook and verify the internet connection. If email synchronization still fails, escalate to Service Desk.
