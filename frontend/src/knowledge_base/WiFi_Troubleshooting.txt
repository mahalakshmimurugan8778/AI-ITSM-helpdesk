Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

Problem:
Unable to connect to the company Wi-Fi.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check that Wi-Fi is turned on.
2. Disconnect and reconnect to the Wi-Fi network.
3. Verify the Wi-Fi password.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. Check whether other devices can connect.
6. If the problem continues, contact Service Desk.

Resolution:
Reconnect to the correct Wi-Fi network and restart the laptop. If the issue continues, escalate to Service Desk.
