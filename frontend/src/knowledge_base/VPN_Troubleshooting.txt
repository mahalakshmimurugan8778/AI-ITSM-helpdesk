VPN Troubleshooting

Problem:
Unable to connect to company VPN. Authentication keeps failing.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Check that your username and password are correct.
2. Make sure your internet connection is working.
3. Restart the VPN client.
4. Check whether your VPN account is locked.
5. Re-enter your VPN credentials.
6. If the problem continues, contact Network Support.

Resolution:
Verify VPN credentials and restart the VPN client. If authentication still fails, escalate the issue to Network Support.
